ABOUT THIS SLIDE
What happens if a state board is appointed anyway. Two lanes. The top lane fills almost at once: a commissioner can replace individual managers at any time. The bottom lane is slow: removing the whole board early requires the triggering campus to earn acceptable ratings in consecutive years, and then it is the commissioner's call.
Source: Texas Education Code sections 39A.207, 39A.208 and 39A.209.

SAY (opening)
Suppose a state board is appointed. A commissioner can swap out individual managers whenever they want. A new commissioner could put different people in charge right away.

[click] Ending the takeover early is different. The campus that triggered it has to earn acceptable ratings in consecutive school years.

[click] Even then, removal is the commissioner's decision. The law sets no fixed date for the elected board to come back.

IF ASKED
How long would a takeover last? The law has a separate process for expiration and a phased return of elected trustees. Houston's state board has been in place since 2023 and is extended through June 2027.
