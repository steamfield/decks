Evidence that the Republican Senate does vote for school money. The big number is HB 2, the 2025 school funding law. The arc shows the Senate vote: 31 to 0. The last beat brings it back to Austin, which still had to cut its budget.
Terms: recapture is the money a property-wealthy district like Austin sends back to the state when its local property taxes exceed what the state formula says it should get.

SAY (opening)
In 2025 the Legislature passed HB 2, about 8.5 billion dollars in new money for public schools over the two-year state budget, including teacher pay and special education.

[click] Every senator voted for it, 31 to 0. Funding is a place the two parties have agreed.

[click] Austin ISD still had to cut. Before its cuts, the district projected a 181 million dollar gap for 2026-27. It also budgeted about 715 million dollars in local property taxes to send back to the state in 2025-26. That is recapture.

IF ASKED
Does the unanimous vote mean vouchers or takeover reform would pass? No. This vote is about funding only. Vouchers and the takeover rule would each need their own bill.
Is the 181 million the current deficit? No. It is the projection before the district's reductions.
